Prüfungssimulation: three ways to re-do the page

Every screen below is drawn from the real app tokens (the s187 “N3 Slate” dark palette, the tighter 8 px corner scale, the shipped card, badge and button recipes). German inside a frame is the app copy under review; everything outside a frame is review text. Pick one option by name, or mix (“B, but with A’s status card”).

What the current page gets wrong

1
It answers nothing

Five “Starten” buttons and no statement of where the learner stands, so the page has no reason to be visited twice.

2
It stops half way down

Content ends at ~45 % of the screen and the rest is empty ground. Results already exist in the data and are shown nowhere.

3
The same fact twice

“4 Teile · 52 Min” on top and four cards each printing their own minutes below it.

4
Loose chrome on top

A “NIVEAU” label plus four floating grey buttons, above a big hero, before anything the learner came for.

Shared in all three options

The Niveau row becomes the sliding-pill switcher already used by Bibliothek and Schreiben, parked beside the title instead of stacked under it (one line saved, one less style in the app). The exam countdown from Settings appears on the page it belongs to, not only in Fortschritt. And a Verlauf block shows the last runs, which is the data that fills the empty half. Flip “First visit” or “A2” above to see the honest empty states.

Option A

Stand zuerst

The page leads with how ready you are.

Desktop

Phone · 390 px · scrolls inside the frame

One status card carries the last total as a ring, the four skills as bars, and the single primary action. The four parts drop to a quiet list underneath, because starting one part is the secondary move.

Cost: the most new surface of the three, and on day one the ring reads “–”. The layout stays the same shape when it is empty, the way Verlauf in Schreiben does, so the page never changes skeleton once results arrive.

Option B

Prüfungstag

The page leads with the run itself.

Desktop

Phone · 390 px · scrolls inside the frame

The full exam becomes one band that visibly contains the four parts as a timeline, so the duplicated minutes disappear and the exam-day shape is the thing you look at. Practising a single part sits below it as “Einzeln üben”.

Cost: the four parts lose their card presence and become rows, and the band is the only place a result is shown as a number rather than a bar.

Option C

Vier Karten

Today’s skeleton, given weight.

Desktop

Phone · 390 px · scrolls inside the frame

Same three blocks as today, but each part card carries its last result as a bar, the full run states the countdown, and Verlauf closes the page.

Cost: the smallest change and the smallest gain. The page still opens with four equal choices instead of one, and the four cards stay text-tight at two columns on a phone.
